# KneeGuide Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and professional project environment for clinicians, patients, engineers, designers, researchers, reviewers and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and evidence.
- Protect privacy and never share real patient or confidential data.
- Be transparent about prototype limitations, uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Avoid unsupported medical, clinical, performance or regulatory claims.
- Respect accessibility, language, cultural and professional differences.
- Accept maintainer decisions intended to protect safety, privacy and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Misrepresenting experimental software as a diagnostic or treatment authority.
- Deliberately weakening security or bypassing access controls.
- Using project channels for spam, unrelated promotion or unsafe medical advice.

## Enforcement

Organization owners may edit, remove or reject content and may restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported privately using the security policy.

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other official KneeGuide project spaces.
