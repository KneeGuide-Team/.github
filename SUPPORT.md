# KneeGuide Support and Project Questions

KneeGuide is an advanced prototype developed for MEDATHON 2026. It is not a clinical service, emergency service or substitute for professional medical care.

## Technical questions

- Review the organization profile and the relevant repository README first.
- Use a repository issue for reproducible bugs, documentation problems or scoped feature discussions.
- Include environment details and synthetic reproduction data.
- Do not include passwords, tokens, OTP values, patient information or production credentials.

## Security reports

Follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md). Do not report vulnerabilities in a public issue.

## Medical questions

Project channels cannot provide individual diagnosis, treatment or rehabilitation instructions. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical guidance and local emergency services for urgent care.
